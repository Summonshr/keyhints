Sites where single keys are off

    There, f and the other single keys are left to the site. Alt+J always works. The toolbar button switches the site you're on.

    Hints

    Lowercase, no repeats, at least 4, easiest first. Labels taken from an element's name can use any letter.

    By site

    Share and back up

    "Copy keymap" under a site copies its labels as text. Pasted labels are added to yours; in a clash the pinned one, then the most recent, wins.

    More

    Palette search Nothing leaves your browser either way.

    Sync: settings and pinned keys go to your other computers when the browser's sync is on. Forgetting a site's labels unpins its keys there too.
