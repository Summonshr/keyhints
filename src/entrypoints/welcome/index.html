KeyHints is on

Click anything on any page without the mouse. Try it right here: it takes 20 seconds.

1. Click without the mouse

Press f (or Alt+J). Every button gets a letter label. Type the label of any button below.

2. Watch it learn

Labels come from each button's name: Explore is e, Messages is m. Pick Home three times (f, then its label); a button you use often keeps its key on that site, even when other buttons start with the same letter.

3. Ask for anything

Press Alt+K and type, or dictate, what you want: "new tab", "close duplicate tabs", "next page", or the name of any button or open tab. Enter runs it.

Search bookmarks and history too?

The palette searches open tabs and the page. It can also search your bookmarks and history, if you allow it. Nothing leaves your browser either way.

Sharing your screen?

Show keys puts the keys you press in the corner, so people watching can follow along. It never shows what you type into fields.

Good to know

  • ? shows every key that works on the page you're on.
  • On YouTube, Gmail and a few other sites with their own single-key shortcuts, f stays theirs. Alt+J always works. The toolbar button switches single keys on or off for the current site.
  • Hands-free: open the palette and dictate. A dictated command runs by itself after a moment.
  • No account, no tracking: KeyHints never makes a network request. Your labels stay in this browser.
